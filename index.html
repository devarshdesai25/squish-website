What Squish does

Compress Images Available

Pick a limit (20, 50, 100, 200, 500 KB or your own) and Squish keeps the best quality that fits.

Resize & Crop Available

Passport photos, signatures and exact sizes in pixels or centimetres, with an optional KB limit.

Images → PDF Available

Turn photos or camera scans into one PDF, in the order you choose.

Unlock PDF Available

Remove the password from a statement or salary slip, using the password you already know.

Lock PDF Available

Protect a PDF with your PAN or your own password (AES-256) before you share it.

Merge PDFs Available

Join up to 20 PDFs into one. Password-protected ones can be unlocked right in the list.

Your files never leave your phone

  • No uploads: every image and PDF is processed on your device.
  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
  • No storage permission: you choose exactly which photos and files Squish sees.
  • PDF passwords and PAN are used only to open or protect your file, and never stored.
